Stellaria

A polyhedra puzzle

App Store · iPhone and iPad Mac App Store

Turn each piece with a tap, launch it into its slot and close the star before the sky fills up. Every star is a stellation of a polyhedron, computed on the device: the geometry is real.

  • 28 polyhedra · 234 stellations · 7 languages
  • Free with four polyhedra, one purchase for the rest · no ads, no accounts, no data collection
  • Made by one developer in Spain
